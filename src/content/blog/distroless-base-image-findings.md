### Why three families cannot reach zero

Red Hat has published no erratum for pcre2 or glibc, and Debian has not rebuilt glibc, libexpat1 or libuuid1. Those floors are vendor-blocked, not accepted risk.

### The Jackson case is the instructive one

Spring Boot 4 runs the 2.x and 3.x Jackson trains in parallel, but its parent manages only the newer one. A routine version bump therefore leaves the legacy train exactly where it was.
